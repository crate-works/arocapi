## Install

```sh
pnpm add arocapi@beta @prisma/client@7 @opensearch-project/opensearch
```

## What you get

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Visibility & Access" icon="seti:lock">
    Your filters decide which entities exist for a request, and your access function decides who may read their
    metadata and files. arocapi applies both on every route. [How it works](/concepts/visibility-vs-access/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pluggable storage" icon="seti:folder">
    Serve RO-Crate metadata and files from local disk, object storage or OCFL by streaming them or redirecting to them.
    [Storage strategies](/strategies/storage/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Search" icon="magnifier">
    Full-text search, filters, facets and sorts from fields you declare once, on OpenSearch or a backend of your own.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
